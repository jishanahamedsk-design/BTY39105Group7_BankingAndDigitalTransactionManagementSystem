1. Introduction and Scope

IBI Bank — International Bank of India ("IBI Bank," "we," "our," or "us") refers in this policy to the operator of this website and its educational application. The identity and legal status of the operator should be confirmed separately where formal legal identification is required.

This Privacy Policy describes the categories of personal information the application may process, the purposes for processing, possible recipients, retention considerations, security measures, and available privacy-request channels.

The application demonstrates features such as customer and administrator access, account records, transaction history, beneficiaries, fund-transfer demonstrations, loan records, EMI management, and administrative operations. Actual processing depends on the features enabled in the deployed version.

This policy applies to the IBI Bank website and associated application services controlled by its operator. It does not automatically govern independent third-party websites, email providers, hosting services, or social-media platforms.

2. Information We May Process

2.1 Personal and customer details

Depending on the forms and features used, the application may process a user's name, email address, telephone number, residential or mailing address, customer identifier, and other details submitted through an enabled feature.

2.2 Authentication and account-access information

Authentication-related information may include a login email address, user identifier, assigned role, account status, password-verification data, login OTP records, password-reset verification records, session or token information, and relevant security events.

OTP records may include an intended recipient, purpose, expiry time, verification-attempt count, and consumption status. A verification code should not be treated as safe to disclose merely because it is sent by email.

2.3 Account and branch records

The application may maintain demonstration account identifiers, account numbers, account types, customer and branch references, account status, recorded balances, and related creation or modification details.

2.4 Transaction and transfer records

Where supported, transaction records may include transaction identifiers, types, amounts, dates, descriptions, source and destination account references, transfer status, and recorded balance changes. Application records are not proof that money moved through a real banking network.

2.5 Beneficiary information

Beneficiary-management features may process beneficiary names, account references or account numbers, status, and details associated with transfer demonstrations. Only use fictional or appropriately authorized test information.

2.6 Loan and EMI information

Loan features may process loan categories, amounts, interest rates, repayment terms, instalment amounts, due dates, payment statuses, outstanding amounts, and associated customer references.

2.7 Technical and operational information

Depending on server, hosting, and application configuration, technical records may include IP addresses, browser and device information, request timestamps, error logs, and diagnostic data. We do not claim that every category is collected in every deployment.

3. How Information Is Collected

  • Application processing: Information read or updated by the backend when an authorized application request is handled.

  • Technical operations: Records generated by the web server, database, hosting environment, or security tools, where configured.
  • Administration: Information created, reviewed, or corrected by an authorized administrator or database operator.

We do not claim to use advertising trackers, analytics platforms, or additional data sources unless those technologies are actually enabled.

Information may be processed for relevant application purposes, including:

Information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and should not be reused for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any notice or consent required by law.

6. Data Storage, Database Access, and Administration

The current development architecture uses a Node.js and Express backend with a MySQL database. In the deployed application, information may be stored in that database or in other configured storage systems.

Database records may cover customers, users, accounts, branches, transactions, beneficiaries, transfers, loans, EMI payments, and authentication verification records.

Appropriate safeguards should include:

  • Restricting database access to authorized services and personnel.
  • Using parameterized SQL queries or prepared statements.
  • Validating and authorizing backend requests.
  • Protecting database credentials and environment files.
  • Using separate development and production configurations where appropriate.
  • Restricting administrative permissions according to job responsibilities.
  • Maintaining appropriate backup, recovery, and deletion procedures.

Describing these safeguards does not mean every measure is already deployed. Actual protections must be verified against the running code, database configuration, hosting provider, and operational practices.

7. Information Security

The operator should use appropriate technical and organizational measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse of information.

  • Password security: Use an appropriate password-hashing algorithm and suitable parameters rather than readable plaintext passwords.
  • Authorization: Check user identity, role, permissions, and ownership on the backend for protected operations.
  • OTP controls: Apply expiry, attempt limits, verification, and single-use handling where implemented.
  • Transport security: Use HTTPS on deployed sites that transmit personal or authentication information.
  • Database security: Restrict database privileges and protect credentials.
  • Session security: Use appropriate token or session expiry, logout, and revocation controls.
  • Safe error handling: Avoid disclosing credentials, OTPs, stack traces, or sensitive internal details to users.
  • Maintenance: Review dependencies, configuration, and relevant vulnerabilities.

No website or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should report suspected unauthorized access or security issues through the contact channel below, without sending passwords or verification codes.

8. Passwords, OTPs, and Sensitive Financial Information

Never enter real banking passwords, ATM or debit-card PINs, card security codes, internet-banking credentials, or other real financial authentication secrets into this demonstration application.

Use fictional accounts and test data wherever possible. Do not reuse passwords from real financial services. Do not share OTPs with anyone, including someone claiming to represent the application operator.

The application may send email OTPs for account verification, login, or password recovery where those workflows are enabled. Never include OTPs or passwords in support emails.

Security claims in this policy are subject to the actual deployed implementation. This policy does not guarantee that every possible security feature exists or that a demonstration account is protected like an account at a regulated bank.

10. Cookies and Browser Storage

The application may use browser storage or cookies to maintain application state, remember preferences, or support authentication and navigation.

The current frontend has used browser localStorage keys such as authToken, loggedInUser, and loggedInAdmin. Actual keys and storage behavior depend on the page and version being used.

Browser storage is different from server-side database storage. Data stored in localStorage can persist after a browser tab closes and may be accessible to JavaScript running on the same origin. A cross-site scripting vulnerability can therefore create risks for tokens stored there.

The operator should assess whether authentication tokens should instead be held in appropriately configured secure, HttpOnly cookies or another suitable session mechanism. This policy does not assert that such a change has already been made.

Clearing browser storage may sign you out or affect application functionality. Browser cookie settings can be used to manage cookies where they are used. We do not claim to use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or cross-site tracking unless those technologies are actually enabled.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its disclosed purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate operational needs.

Retention may differ by information type:

  • User and customer records may remain until an authorized deletion or account-management action occurs.
  • Transaction and related account records may remain to preserve application history and integrity.
  • OTP records may be retained according to verification, expiry, security, and cleanup procedures.
  • Technical logs may follow server or hosting retention settings.
  • Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup-retention period expires.

The operator should define and implement actual retention periods. This policy does not promise that every record is immediately deleted when a request is submitted.

Deleting a visible record may not remove related database entries, transaction history, logs, or backups. Such information may need separate handling, subject to integrity requirements and applicable law.
